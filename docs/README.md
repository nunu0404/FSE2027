Four files are in Korean, as written. They are the provenance record for the
interval and recomputation work; their conclusions are restated in English in
`../CLAIMS_TO_ARTIFACTS.md`.

These reports describe the runs as they happened, including arms that were
blocked or excluded. Where a report and the manuscript differ in framing, the
manuscript is the claim of record and `../CLAIMS_TO_ARTIFACTS.md` is the map.
